# PathFinder India - 2-Minute Video Demo Script

**Goal:** Fit the core problem, the reactive constraint graph, bilingual support, AI explanation, and the Action Plan into a tight 120-second window.

---

### ⏱️ 0:00 - 0:15 | The Problem & Intake
**[Screen]** Start on the Landing Page. Click English to Hindi toggle. 
**[Voiceover]** "Millions of Indian students make career choices blindly because they lack access to counseling and cannot navigate complex English government websites. PathFinder India is a bilingual, reactive constraint map that shows students exactly what doors their choices open or close."
**[Action]** Switch back to English, click "Start Your Journey". 
**[Action]** Quickly fill Intake: *Class 12 -> Commerce (Without Maths) -> Low Budget -> Long term earning urgency*.

### ⏱️ 0:15 - 0:45 | The Hero Screen & Live Stats
**[Screen]** The Pathway Map loads.
**[Voiceover]** "This is the Pathway Workspace. Instead of hunting through PDFs, students see their entire career timeline—Immediate, Next, and Future. Notice the Live Statistics bar at the top: because we chose 'Without Maths' and a 'Low Budget', you can see instantly how many high-tier paths are completely closed off."
**[Action]** Scroll through the columns briefly, highlighting the red "CLOSED" and green "OPEN" badges.

### ⏱️ 0:45 - 1:15 | The "What If" Simulator (Reactive Graph)
**[Screen]** The Sticky Parameter Bar at the top.
**[Voiceover]** "The core innovation is the Reactive Constraint Graph. Watch what happens if this student manages to secure an education loan, moving their budget from 'Low' to 'High'."
**[Action]** Change Budget dropdown from "Low" to "High".
**[Screen]** *Wait for the Cascade Toast to pop up.*
**[Voiceover]** "The engine instantly re-evaluates every edge in the graph. The toast notification shows us exactly how many new doors just opened. We can see private colleges and premium courses are now unlocked."

### ⏱️ 1:15 - 1:40 | AI Guidance & Recovery Routes
**[Screen]** Scroll to a node marked "CLOSED" (e.g., B.Tech Computer Science).
**[Action]** Click "✨ Ask AI for Guidance". 
**[Voiceover]** "When a student hits a closed door, our AI Explanation layer steps in. It analyzes their constraints—in this case, missing Mathematics—and explains the exact trade-offs and next steps. It even maps out 'Recovery Routes', like registering for the NIOS math exam, turning a 'Closed' door into a 'Reopenable' one."
**[Action]** *Optional: If API key modal pops up, mention that judges can easily plug in their own OpenAI/Groq keys directly in the UI if the server quota runs out.*

### ⏱️ 1:40 - 2:00 | Compare & WhatsApp Action Plan
**[Screen]** Close AI modal. Click checkboxes on 2-3 different open pathways.
**[Action]** Click "Compare" on the bottom floating bar. Briefly show the matrix.
**[Action]** Close Compare, click "Get Action Plan".
**[Voiceover]** "Finally, the student selects their target paths and generates a 30-Day Action Plan. Because digital literacy varies, they can instantly share this exact checklist with their parents or teachers via WhatsApp. This is PathFinder India—career clarity for everyone."
**[Action]** Click the WhatsApp share button (shows the native WhatsApp deep-link). 
**[Screen]** Fade to black.
